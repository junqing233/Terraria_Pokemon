模组目前添加了14种宝可梦，每种宝可梦可升到100级，升到一定等级可以学会新的技能。

宝可梦的技能伤害受到等级影响，还受玩家手持武器面板伤害影响，具体形式后面还会进行调整的喵。

模组还添加了一些宝可梦玩偶和其他道具，可以在宝箱中找到它们。

模组还设计了一些以宝可梦为主题的武器，欢迎体验并发表你的宝贵想法。

"I am striving to become a Pokémon Master!"

The mod has added 14 different Pokémon, each capable of leveling up to 100. As they reach certain levels, they can learn new moves.

You can find these adorable creatures in some corners of the world, and then throw a Poké Ball to catch them!

The damage of Pokémon moves is influenced by their level and the damage of the weapon panel held by the player. The specific form will be adjusted later, neow.

The mod also includes some Pokémon plushies and other items, which can be found in treasure chests.

Some weapons themed around Pokémon have been designed in the mod. Feel free to try them and share your valuable thoughts.
